# Elder Mirou is the quest giver: what he says depends on the quests, see
# assets/quests/quest_dialogue.txt.

[gardener]
Oh! Hello there! Careful where you step, the petals are still sleeping. I'm Hana, I look after the sakura trees.
Have you met the little spirit that lives in the giant tree? It loves visitors. Walk close to the tree and it will come and say hello.
If you are patient, it may even leave you a small golden gift.
And here is a secret, just between us... On the far side of the island, away from the portal, someone hid a shining diamond near the shore.
It sits behind a curved wall of big rocks. You won't see it from the middle of the island: walk along the shore and come at it from the sea side!
Now go on, and say hi to the pink slimes for me. They love to follow people around.

[explorer]
Brr! Hello, friend! Rook's the name, exploring's the game. Mind the snow, it gets everywhere.
The slimes here are terribly shy. Walk toward them and they hop away as fast as they can. Approach slowly if you want a closer look.
Want to hear about my greatest discovery? To the south of this island, on the side opposite the portal, a tiny rock floats all by itself. I call it Sky Rock.
It's too far to jump, I tried, twice! But with your glider, it's an easy hop: run off the southern shore and hold Space.
They say a rainbow diamond waits on top. When you're done, just walk off the edge: the wind brings you back here.

[scholar]
Ah, a visitor! Fascinating. I am Professor Lumen, and I study the crystals of this island.
Do you see the great crystal column in the middle? Its flat top holds a rare diamond. Scientifically speaking, it is extremely shiny.
Six crystal steps spiral up around the column. Each one is exactly one unit higher than the one before, which is just low enough to jump.
My advice: jump from step to step at walking speed. No need to run, the steps are close together.
You will also find purple amethysts all over the island. I have catalogued five of them... but I keep losing count.
And beware: the purple slimes are cautious. Come too close and they will hop away, offended.

# ---- At night ----

[gardener.night]
Oh, you're up late too? The sakura look so soft under the moon...
Look at the fireflies dancing between the trees! They only come out when the sun is asleep.
The hidden diamond is still waiting on the far side of the island, behind the curved wall of rocks. Bring a lantern... or just follow the fireflies!

[explorer.night]
Brr! The nights on Frost island are something else. Look up: you can see every star from here!
Did you know the stars turn slowly around the sky during the night? I've been mapping them.
If you glide to Sky Rock in the dark, aim for the moonlight on the snow. Watch your step!

[scholar.night]
Ah, a nocturnal visitor! The crystals hum differently at night. Fascinating.
The moon gives a cold blue light: perfect to admire the crystal column. Its six steps are still one unit apart, even in the dark.
Do be careful: the shadows do not sleep at night, and neither, apparently, do I.
