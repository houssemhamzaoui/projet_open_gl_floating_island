# =============================================================================
#  Floating Island - what Elder Mirou says about the quests
#
#  Same format as assets/dialogue/npcs.txt. For each quest id:
#    [id.offer]      when the quest is available (accepted when the dialogue ends)
#    [id.progress]   while it is active, objectives not done yet
#    [id.ready]      when every objective is done (turned in when the dialogue ends)
#    [id.done]       after it was turned in, if no other quest is available
#  And:
#    [elder.later]   nothing to give right now, and no [id.done] to say
# =============================================================================

[first_steps.offer]
Ah, a new face on the Floating Isles! Welcome, little traveler. I am Mirou.
I have walked these islands for longer than the clouds can remember. Behind me stand the five portals: the color of each swirl shows where it leads.
Purple goes to the Crystal island, gray-green to the Rocky Islet, amber to the Cave, icy blue to the Frost island, and pink to Sakura. Every island has a green portal back to this plaza.
Before anything else, go and see the world! Visit Sakura, Frost, Crystal and the Cave island, then come back and tell me about it.

[first_steps.progress]
The islands are waiting for you. Sakura, Frost, Crystal and the Cave: walk on each of them.
Remember: the portals behind me take you there in a blink. Or jump off an edge and hold Space to glide, if you like the wind.

[first_steps.ready]
You have seen all four? Wonderful! I can see the sky in your eyes.
Here, take this: a Sky Ribbon. Tie it to your glider and the wind will leave a glowing trail behind you.

[petal_collector.offer]
Now, a favor for an old friend. Hana, the gardener of Sakura, lost her pink sakura gems in the wind.
Five of them are scattered across her island. Could you find them for her?

[petal_collector.progress]
Hana's pink gems shine softly among the petals of Sakura Island. Five of them, if my memory serves.

[petal_collector.ready]
Five pink gems! Hana will be so happy.
She asked me to give you this in return: petal wings for your glider. You'll fly like a blossom in spring.

[voices.offer]
My three friends live on the other islands, and they know secrets even I have forgotten.
Hana tends the sakura trees, Rook explores the snow of Frost island, and Professor Lumen studies the crystals. Go and say hello to each of them.

[voices.progress]
Have you met everyone? Hana on Sakura, Rook on Frost, and Professor Lumen on the Crystal island. They usually wait near the portals.

[voices.ready]
So you have heard their stories. They all liked you, I can tell.
Rook sent these for you: Swift Boots. You'll run like the wind, and leave a little gold behind you.

[voices.done]
That is all I have for you today, little traveler.
But lately, I have felt shadows moving at the edges of the islands... Come back later. I may need your help.

[shadows.offer]
Dark creatures prowl the islands now. Shadows with red eyes, that bite the hands that come close.
Please, chase five of them away. Be careful, and come back to me if you get hurt.

[shadows.progress]
The shadows still prowl at the edges of the islands. Five of them, my brave friend.

[shadows.ready]
The islands breathe more easily already. Thank you.
Take this Heart of Courage. It will make you stronger for what comes next.

[shadows.done]
Something stirs deep under the Cave mountain. I can feel it in my old bones. Come back later.

[cave_guardian.offer]
The Cave Heart... An ancient guardian of rock and crystal protects it, deep in the chamber. Korvath, they called it.
It sleeps like a statue, and a crystal seal keeps the Heart out of reach. But if someone walks into its chamber with a brave heart... it wakes.
Watch it closely. When its arms rise, jump. When it crouches and scrapes the floor, step aside. When the floor glows, move!
Defeat the guardian and bring the Heart back to the light. This will be your greatest adventure.

[cave_guardian.progress]
The guardian waits in the crystal chamber. Watch its movements: it always warns before it strikes.
Arms up: jump over the shockwave. Crouching: dodge the charge, and strike while it is dazed against the wall. Glowing floor: get out of the circles!

[cave_guardian.ready]
You did it! The Cave Heart, free at last!
The islands will remember you. Wear this crown of crystals: you have earned it, hero of the Floating Isles.

[cave_guardian.done]
Rest now, hero. The islands are at peace, and the clouds sing your name.

[elder.later]
There is nothing more I can ask of you for now, little traveler. Come back later!
